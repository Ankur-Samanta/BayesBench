BayesBench · Multi-turn belief updating

Do language models update their beliefs rationally?

Each turn provides new evidence that should reduce epistemic uncertainty about the environment. BayesBench asks how closely LLM belief updates match those of a rational Bayesian reasoner as evidence accumulates.

02 / What we found

Better latent inference does not yet translate into calibrated downstream prediction.

Finding 01

↑ latent
inference
does not guarantee
↑ downstream
accuracy

Models infer latent structure from accumulating evidence, and scaling strengthens this inference. The gains do not reliably carry over to downstream prediction.

Finding 02

Bayesian target

Evidence accumulates.
Calibration lags.

Models are capable of Bayesian-aligned evidence accumulation, but do not reliably track the Bayesian reference. Smaller models adapt too weakly; larger models can over-update toward the extremes.

Finding 03

PassiveActive← pro-user shift

Active engagement can bias evidence accumulation.

In social judgment, active engagement reveals a persistent pro-user bias relative to passive observation of the same evidence.

03 / Selected results

Beliefs in motion.

Intermediate belief trajectories expose departures from rational belief updating that final-turn accuracy alone can miss.

Environment 01

Coin flip

Trajectories overshoot or undershoot the Bayesian posterior across scales; larger models can over-update toward the extremes.

Environment 02

Recommendation

Larger models recover the latent user type more accurately, but still struggle to turn that inference into reliably accurate rating predictions.

Environment 03a

Social judgment

Active engagement shifts beliefs toward NTA relative to passive observation, lowering error on NTA cases and raising it on YTA cases.

Environment 03b

Medical triage

Models infer the patient’s communication style, but fall short of reliably using it to calibrate urgency decisions.

The takeaway

Models can identify relevant hidden structure, but cannot yet reliably translate that inference into calibrated predictions.
